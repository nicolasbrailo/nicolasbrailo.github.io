Work Accounts

Create and manage accounts for team members using Facebook Work Accounts. Work Accounts are a new account type designed for business use, so your employees don't have to log in to Portal with their personal Facebook accounts.

Device management

The Portal Device Manager makes it easy to deploy and manage Portal devices at scale. You can control your Work Accounts, device settings, see device status, reboot or factory reset any Portal and more – from wherever you are.

DISCOVER. SHARE. CREATE.

Step inside Workrooms and discover VR tools and features that can boost your team's productivity.
SHARED WHITEBOARD

PUT PEN TO WHITEBOARD

Ideate and brainstorm on whiteboards that you can return to at any time in VR—or export them to the web. Create a hands-on experience by holding your controller like a pen and writing directly on your physical desk or a vertical surface in your room.
MIXED-REALITY DESK

BYOD: BRING YOUR OWN DESK

Bring your physical desk into the VR space. Workrooms blends your home office seamlessly with the VR environment, so you can feel immersed while still grounded and comfortable.
INSTALL REMOTE DESKTOP
COMPUTER IN VR

CLICK RIGHT INTO WORK

Bring your desktop front and center. Pair your computer to type, take notes, and share your screen easily with Oculus Remote Desktop.*
CUSTOMIZABLE ROOMS

MAKE THE SPACE YOUR OWN

Express yourself and establish your team’s culture with custom logos and posters in your meeting room.

HAND TRACKING
Talk, turn, and listen
Immerse yourself as you hear voices from around the table, with volume adjusting automatically depending on their location in VR.
Come as yourself
Choose a custom avatar that’s an authentic representation of you. Nod, turn, and be yourself in Workrooms.
Talk with your hands
With hand tracking technology, use your hands to point, type, or give a thumbs-up with a hands-first experience.
VIDEO CALL PARTICIPATION

No headset? No problem

Workrooms makes VR conferencing easy for everyone. Use your laptop or desktop computer to dial into Workrooms through video call and see the full room, interact with your teammates' avatars, and share your screen.
WEB APP

Flow between 2D and VR

Workrooms allows you to easily collaborate in headset and on desktop. Share your screen to VR participants from desktop, save a shared whiteboard to web, and send chats, take notes, and assign action items synchronously in and out of VR.

BRING YOUR TOOLS AND YOUR TEAM

Workroom gives you the right tools for working from home. Do more together and start exploring Workrooms (beta) with your team today.

Generate leads through phone calls.

Lead ads with calling make it easy for potential customers to contact you over the phone to learn more about products or services, schedule an appointment or place an order.

Meta supports different ways to use phone calls for lead generation. You can allow people to call you directly from an ad after clicking through to your website, or give leads the option to request a call back from you in the future.

Call ads are now even more performant.

Recent updates to the call ads user experience reduced cost and increased conversion rate for high-value phone calls. Changes included microphone permissions handling when calling on iOS, allowing calls to be made from new ad entry points, and one-tap calling for high-calling-intent entry points.

Get started with callback requests.

  • Objective: Leads
  • Conversion location: Calls
  • Destination: Provide the phone number that customers should call to contact you. Tick the Request a callback box to receive callback requests via Messenger.
  • Experience: Potential customer provides a day, time and phone number to be contacted on. Outbound calls happen via Messenger for continuity.
  • Note: Eligible businesses will now automatically receive email notifications when customers submit callback requests.

  • Conversion location: Website

Create a call ad campaign in a variety of ways.

Full call ad functionality

Recommended for most advertisers. Create a new call ad in Meta Ads Manager or the Ads Manager app to access full product capabilities, including ad scheduling.

Simpler call ad functionality

Create a simpler call ad from your Facebook Page with new creative or by boosting an existing post, or in the Meta Business Suite app by selecting Get more calls as your goal. If you want to schedule your ad to run during certain hours, create your call ad from Ads Manager.

Marketing API from Meta

Third-party developers can easily create and publish call ads using the Marketing API.

Improve setup

Use these principles to improve campaign performance.

Adopt a mixed lead ad strategy.

If you schedule call ad campaigns to run during certain hours, complement with an instant form or lead ads that click to message campaign that runs outside of business hours, or 24/7, so prospects can get in touch at any time.

Optimise with automation and AI.

Calling campaigns using the Leads objective can leverage Advantage+ single-step solutions for audience, placement, budget and creative to improve performance and save time and resources.

Set up conversion attribution.

Learn which campaigns are driving valuable inbound calls by choosing your attribution setting at the ad set level in Ads Manager, to track website events (set up Meta pixel), app events (integrate with Facebook Software Development Kit) and offline events (create an offline event set in Events Manager).

Set up best practices

Adopt these strategies to improve campaign performance.

View call ad reporting metrics in Ads Manager.

By creating a call ad in Meta Ads Manager or on your Facebook Page, you can view metrics that are specific to those call ads.

  • Estimated call confirmation clicks: The estimated number of times the call button on a call confirmation dialogue is clicked from an ad with a call destination.
  • Calls placed: The number of times a call was made to your business by selecting an ad.
  • 20-second calls: The number of times that calls placed resulted in successful connections lasting at least 20 seconds.
  • 60-second calls: The number of times that calls placed resulted in successful connections lasting at least 60 seconds.
  • Callback requests submitted: The number of times people have successfully messaged your business to request a call using the Send message button on your call ad.

Frequently asked questions

Expand allCollapse all

Where will my call ads run?

Call ads can be delivered across the following ad placements on mobile devices only: Facebook Feed, Facebook Marketplace, Facebook Reels, Facebook Video feed, Instagram Feed and Instagram Stories.

How does reporting work for call ads?

Regardless of objective and performance goal, call ad campaigns will deliver an aggregated report of customer calls placed, connected calls and 60-second connected calls.

Does a call ad work with a mobile phone number or landlines?

Call ads can run with any phone number the business provides, including 1-800 and toll-free numbers.

When should I use a lifetime budget and when should I use a daily budget?

You should use a lifetime budget if you want to use call ads with the ad scheduling feature and schedule it to run during select hours. If you do not need to use ad scheduling, then you can use a daily budget for call ads as well.

How are advertisers charged for call ads?

Advertisers are charged on cost-per-impressions (CPM).

Is dynamic creative available for call ads?

Dynamic creative optimisation for call ads is not currently available.
